# Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) Aviation MRO Tooling & Hardware Cargo Pipeline

## Hub Logistics Architecture: Los Angeles (LAX) to GCC Civil Aviation Gateways

### Operational Protocols for Aviation Importers
- **Compliant Classification**: EAR99 export verification for aircraft maintenance hand tools, torque wrenches, and pneumatic riveters.
- **Airworthiness Tag Integrity**: FAA Form 8130-3 and EASA Form 1 certificates inspected and sealed in weatherproof pouches.
- **Direct Tariffs**: $8.50/kg actual gross scale weight for shipments ≥10kg.
- **Customs Pre-Clearance**: 5% GCC unified duty and destination VAT pre-settled via DDP.
