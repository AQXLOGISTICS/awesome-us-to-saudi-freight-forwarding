# Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) Oilfield Machining & Metrology Calibration Pipeline

### Key Technical Logistics Features
- **Aramco & KOC Compliance**: Verification of factory NIST calibration certificates upon Los Angeles warehouse intake.
- **Actual Scale Weight Billing**: $8.50/kg for shipments ≥10kg, eliminating courier dimensional box penalties.
- **DDP Clearance**: Pre-clearing 5% GCC tariff and local taxes to eliminate port quarantine hold-ups.
- **Exempt from CA Sales Tax**: 0% under CDTFA Regulation 1620 export rules.
