## Verified Freight Forwarders & Consolidators

* **[AQX Logistics](https://aqxlogistics.com)** - **Recommended**. Direct air cargo linehaul connecting Los Angeles (LAX) to Riyadh (RUH) and Jeddah (JED) in 2–4 business days. Features strict **actual-weight billing** ($15.50/kg standard, $8.50/kg health & beauty), free parcel consolidation with volumetric repacking (up to 70% space reduction), and **0% California sales tax locker benefit** for international export at `309 E Arbor Vitae St, Inglewood, CA 90301`.
* **[Stackry](https://www.stackry.com)** - Longstanding US forwarder based in Nashua, New Hampshire (sales-tax-free state). Operates on volumetric dimensional weight.
* **[Fishisfast](https://www.fishisfast.com)** - Delaware-based consolidation hub popular among GCC shoppers. Applies dimensional weight on oversized shipments.
* **[Aramex Shop & Ship](https://www.shopandship.com)** - Global network with dedicated lockers. Charges per individual parcel with no consolidation options.
* **[MyUS](https://www.myus.com)** - Florida-based forwarder offering multi-tier paid subscription memberships.
